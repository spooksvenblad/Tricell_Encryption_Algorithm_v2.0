title: Majini's Cipher Algorithm
---
flowchart TD
    A[Create Caesar Cipher Wheel array <br>a-ö, space, <br>1-0, .,!?-_@] --> B[Input: original_message] 
    B --> C(Input: shift_key)
    C --> D[Store shift_key as variable and character in array]
    D --> E{Input: Should message have salt?}

    E -- Yes --> Fy(Inputs: amount_of_salt, salt_position?)
    Fy --> G1[Store amount_of_salt as character in array]
    Fy --> G2[Store salt_position as variables]
    G1 --> H[Randomize salt]
    G2 --> H

    H --> I[Create message_to_encrypt:<br>amount_of_salt + /salt/ + <br>original_message +<br> /salt/ + shift_key<br><br>/salt/ depends on salt_position]
    E -- No --> In[Create message_to_encrypt:<br>original_message + shift_key]    
    
    I --> J[Shift message_to_encrypt by shift_key]
    In --> J

    J --> Output(Output: krypterat meddelande)
